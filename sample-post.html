A proof of Cauchy–Schwarz

Sample post. Copy this file to write a new one.

Theorem. In an inner product space, $|\langle u,v\rangle|\le\|u\|\,\|v\|$.

Proof. If $v=0$ both sides are zero. Otherwise, for every scalar $t$, $$0\le\|u-tv\|^2=\|u\|^2-2t\,\mathrm{Re}\langle u,v\rangle+t^2\|v\|^2 .$$ Choose $t=\mathrm{Re}\langle u,v\rangle/\|v\|^2$ (after rotating $v$ by a unit scalar so that $\langle u,v\rangle$ is real); the right side becomes $\|u\|^2-\langle u,v\rangle^2/\|v\|^2\ge 0$. $\qquad\blacksquare$
