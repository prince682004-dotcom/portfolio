About

I am an M.Sc. Mathematics student at IIT Bombay (2025–), preparing for PhD admissions. I qualified IIT JAM Mathematics twice and finished with an all-India rank of 73 in 2025.

My coursework spans real and functional analysis, measure theory, algebra and Galois theory, topology, differential equations and numerical analysis. Earlier I wrote a research project on systems of linear equations and worked on a formula for the envelope of a family of cubics.
